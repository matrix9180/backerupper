# rsync filter rules for backup.sh, relative to $HOME.
#   "- pattern"  exclude      "+ pattern"  include (must come before a broader "-")
#   leading "/"  anchored at $HOME      trailing "/"  directories only
# Rule of thumb: skip caches, reinstallable toolchains, and games. Keep anything
# you wrote yourself or that holds credentials or settings.
#
# Everything NOT listed here is backed up: all dotfiles, .config, .ssh,
# .local/share/keyrings, Projects, media, Pictures, Documents, Desktop, ...
